FFmpeg Notice and Licensing Information
========================================

This application distributes precompiled binary executables of FFmpeg and FFprobe
(version 7.0.2-full_build-www.gyan.dev) as external sidecar processes.

1. Separation and Architecture
------------------------------
Clip Squeezer does NOT link directly against FFmpeg libraries (libavcodec, libavformat, etc.).
All media operations are executed strictly via separate operating system child processes
using standard command-line interfaces and input/output pipes.
